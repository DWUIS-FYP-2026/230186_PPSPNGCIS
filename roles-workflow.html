1. User Roles & Responsibilities

System Administrator

Purpose: Maintains the PMS platform, user accounts, institutions, system settings, and audit integrity across PNGCS and DJAG.

Modules: Overview, Users, Institutions, Prisoners, Applications, Notifications, Audit Log, Settings, Reports, All Forms (view/edit)
CRUD: Full create/read/update/delete on users, institutions, prisoners, and applications (all institutions)
Notifications: Parole eligibility alerts (system-wide), application events
Reports: System, operational, audit, institutional, board statistics

Restrictions: Does not participate in parole decisions unless acting as override; all actions are audit-logged.

CS Parole Clerk

Purpose: Registers prisoners, monitors eligibility, prepares Forms 1–2, coordinates Form 3, and submits complete application packages to DJAG.

Modules: Overview, Prisoners, Eligibility, Applications, Notifications, Reports, Forms 1–2 (edit), Forms 3–5 (view)
Tasks: Prisoner registration (SSD/SED), eligibility verification, Form 1 & 2 completion, submit to DJAG, resubmit after correction
Notifications: Parole eligibility alerts, application returned for correction, application status
Reports: Operational, prisoner, application statistics (institution-scoped)

Scope: Institution-scoped prisoner and application data only.

DJAG Parole Clerk

Purpose: Reviews submitted applications, conducts pre-parole investigation (Form 4), schedules hearings, and forwards cases to the Parole Board.

Modules: Overview, Applications, Pre-Parole Reports, Hearings, Notifications, Form 4 (edit), Forms 1–3 & 5 (view)
Tasks: Accept/review submissions, return for correction, complete Form 4, schedule hearings, send to board
Notifications: New application submitted, hearing scheduled
Reports: Application pipeline, pre-parole, hearing schedules

Parole Board

The Board comprises three members who collectively review applications, evaluate the parolee, and record the final parole decision:

  • DJAG Secretary — Presides over hearings, schedules dates, records the official decision, and signs Forms 4–5
  • PNGCS Commissioner — Provides correctional perspective on conduct and rehabilitation
  • Psychiatrist — Assesses medical and mental health suitability for parole
Modules: Overview, Applications, Hearings, Decisions, Reports, Forms 4–5 (DJAG Secretary)
Decisions: Each member submits Approve · Deny · Defer; DJAG Secretary finalizes the outcome
