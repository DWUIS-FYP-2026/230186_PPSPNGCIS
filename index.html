Welcome to the Parole Management System

A secure digital platform supporting the fair, transparent, and accountable administration of parole for detainees at Bomana Correctional Institution, Port Moresby.

About the Parole System

Parole is a structured reintegration pathway that allows eligible offenders to serve the remainder of their sentence under community supervision, subject to conditions set by the Parole Board. The process balances public safety with rehabilitation and the opportunity for offenders to rebuild their lives responsibly.

The Parole Management System (PMS) digitizes the end-to-end parole workflow — from eligibility screening and application submission through institutional reporting, legal review, board hearings, and final decisions. It provides role-based access for PNGCS, DJAG, and Parole Board staff at every stage of the process.

  • Eligibility screening and automated notifications
  • Structured Forms 1–5 workflow and audit trail
  • Hearing scheduling and board decision records
  • Port Moresby (NCD) institution scope — Bomana

Our Partner Agencies

Parole administration in Papua New Guinea is a collaborative effort between correctional services and the justice sector. Both agencies work together through PMS to ensure every application is processed thoroughly and in accordance with the law.

PNG Correctional Service

PNGCS

PNGCS manages Bomana Correctional Institution in Port Moresby, overseeing the custody, welfare, and rehabilitation of offenders. Through PMS, PNGCS parole clerks initiate eligibility screening, manage detainee records, and prepare institutional reports that inform parole decisions.

  • Parole eligibility screening (Form 1)
  • Offender particulars and applications (Form 2)
  • Institutional conduct reports (Form 3)
  • Port Moresby detainee and case management

Department of Justice & Attorney General

DJAG

DJAG provides legal oversight of the parole process, ensuring applications meet statutory requirements before they proceed to the Parole Board. DJAG parole clerks review submissions, coordinate pre-parole investigations, and support hearing administration and board deliberations.

  • Legal review of submitted applications
  • Pre-parole investigation reports (Form 4)
  • Hearing scheduling and case coordination
  • Parole Board decision records (Form 5)

Working Together for Safer Communities

From the moment an offender becomes eligible for parole, PNGCS and DJAG collaborate through a defined workflow — each agency contributing its expertise at the right stage until the Parole Board reaches a final decision.

  1. 1
    PNGCS — Eligibility & Application

    Screen detainees, notify stakeholders, and submit parole applications with offender particulars.

  2. 2
    PNGCS — Institutional Report

    PNGCS parole clerks provide conduct, program participation, and institutional recommendations.

  3. 3
    DJAG — Legal Review & Investigation

    Review applications for compliance and prepare pre-parole investigation reports.

  4. 4
    Parole Board — Hearing & Decision

    Board members deliberate and record approved, deferred, or refused outcomes.
